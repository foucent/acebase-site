## 📘 Protoss Strategy Guide: Season 1 Fundamentals

*(Notes from Coach JunMa's Masterclass)*

### Lesson 1: PvZ & PvT Core Dynamics

#### I. PvZ Strategic Summary (Countering Zerg Fast Economy / Delayed Tech)

* **Mineral Gathering Micro:** Build Probes continuously. Box 4 Probes and rally them to a single mineral patch. While they are moving, manually split 2 of them to alternative patches; the remaining 2 will split automatically. This requires persistent practice to master.
* **Scouting Routes:** Scout the 3, 6, 9, and 12 o'clock expansions to intercept the Zerg Overlord early. Keep scouting efficient by using only a single Probe.
* **Opening Adaptations:** After placing your Pylon at the choke, if your scouting Probe reaches the Zerg base, observe their opener. If they go for a Fast Expand (Naked Hatchery first), mirror it with a Fast Expand. If they go Pool first, drop your Forge (BF) immediately. Pull a Probe to your Pylon in advance to maximize defensive efficiency.
* **Scouting Probe Micro:** Keep your scouting Probe alive in the Zerg main as long as possible to count Zergling numbers. If you notice only 1–2 Zerglings chasing your Probe and the rest are missing, instantly add a second Photon Cannon (BC) and pull 3–5 Probes to your Natural expansion to hold the position.
* **Countering Delayed Tech / Eco-Heavy Zerg (e.g., Hydralisk Swarm):**
1. **Gas Spending Priority:** Stargate (VS) $\rightarrow$ Ground Weapons +1 upgrade $\rightarrow$ Citadel of Adun (VC, delay upgrades for now) $\rightarrow$ Templar Archives (VT, prioritize High Templar Storm tech).
2. **Exploiting Zerg Overlord Speed Deficit:** Prioritize producing 3 Corsairs + 1 Dark Templar (DT). Use the 3 Corsairs to hunt Overlords while the 1 DT secures your fast Third Base to boost your economy.
3. **Harassment Strategy:** Against low-tech Zerg, expect higher Zergling/Hydralisk counts. Use your +1 Attack Leg-Zealots to map-control and poke for openings. Avoid forcing direct fights that lose army value; play guerrilla style to deny the Zerg's 4th Hatchery. Transition into a frontal push around 150–160 supply with High Templar (Psionic Storm) + Dragoons.
4. **Frontal Combat Requirement:** You must have at least 4 High Templars ready before engaging frontally; attacking with pure Zealot/Dragoon compositions will result in severe losses.
5. **Macro Management:** Do not panic when money accumulates. Keep adding Gateways (BG)—aim for 8–10 Gateways by the 8–10 minute mark. Whenever you have spare APM and minerals, build Gateways and Pylons. Arrange Photon Cannons (BC) around your 3rd and 4th bases in a semi-circle defensive grid.



#### II. PvT Strategic Summary (Countering Terran Bio, Mech Siege & SCV Rushes)

* **Standard Assembly Opener:** 8 Pylon $\rightarrow$ 10 Gateway $\rightarrow$ 11 Assimilator $\rightarrow$ 13 Cybernetics Core (BY, immediately upgrade Singularity Charge for Dragoon range) $\rightarrow$ Produce Dragoons continuously.
* **Scouting Timing:** Send a Probe to scout right after dropping the Cybernetics Core at 13 supply. Locate the Terran position and check if they are securing a Command Center at the Natural. If they have not expanded, prepare for a major 1-Base Rush—immediately scale up to 3 or 4 Gateways to defend.
* **Wall-In Adaptation:** Use Pylons to narrow down the choke point at your Natural expansion, leaving just enough clearance for Dragoons to pass through. This blocks Terran Vulture run-bys (Spider Mines).
* **Dragoon Containment Micro:** Once the first Dragoon spawns, rally it to the Terran front. Set your Gateway rally points directly onto this leading Dragoon so subsequent units follow automatically. Deploy your Dragoons in a horizontal line at the Terran choke (chokepoint or Natural bridge). When Marines push out, kite backwards—fire a shot, pull back, and hit 'H' (Hold Position). Micro heavily damaged Dragoons straight back to base to preserve your army count.
* **Countering All-Ins:** If a Terran rush is detected, drop a Robotics Facility (VR) immediately for Observers (OB). If the Terran army hits your Natural gate and your Dragoon count is insufficient, pull Probes into the fight without hesitation. Sacrificing Probes to hold the rush guarantees a massive mid-game advantage.
* **Post-Defense Transition:** After securing the hold, expand to your 3rd base. Use Pylons to construct a wall blocking both sides of the 3rd to shut down Vulture counter-attacks. Macro up to 10–12 Gateways and maintain Pylon production.
* **Mid-Game Engagement Tactics:**
1. **Army Composition:** After establishing 2 control groups of Dragoons, shift production entirely to Zealots. Spread Observers across the map to secure vision and intercept Terran pushes early. Maintain an production advantage relative to the Terran Factory count (Protoss Gateways $\approx$ Terran Factories + 2).
2. **Natural Choke Defense:** Always leave a few Dragoons stationed at the Natural choke point to intercept rogue Vultures.
3. **Microing the Big Clash (4–5 Control Groups):** Split your 2 Dragoon control groups to the left and right flanks, placing your Zealots in the center. Order the Dragoons to attack-move (A-move) behind the Terran lines; let the Dragoons bait the initial Siege Tank volleys. Simultaneously, charge 2–3 groups of Zealots from the middle. Once close, issue the A-move command. Direct one group of Zealots via right-click deep into the backline straight onto the Siege Tanks to defuse and trigger Spider Mines effectively.

## 📜 Lesson 2: PvP Mirror Matchups

### I. Common PVP Openings

1. **1 Gateway $\rightarrow$ Fast Robotics Facility (VR) Reaver Tech Opener.**
2. **1 Gateway $\rightarrow$ Fast Templar Archives (VT) Dark Templar Opener.**
3. **2 Gateway $\rightarrow$ 3-Zealot Aggression Opener** (designed to pick off enemy Probes before transitioning to macro).
4. **2 Gateway $\rightarrow$ 5-Zealot Hard All-in / Pressure** into Forge (BF) + Natural Expansion.

### II. Deep Dive: 2 Gateway / 3-Zealot Aggressive Opener

* **Tactical Build Order:**
8 Pylon (BP) near the choke point for faster unit rally $\rightarrow$ 10 Gateway (1BG) $\rightarrow$ 12 Gateway (2BG) $\rightarrow$ Immediate cross-scout.
* *Note: Cross-scouting intercepts proxy-Gateway cheese. If the enemy isn't at the direct cross-position, check the remaining spawn spots.*
13 Pylon $\rightarrow$ 15 Assimilator $\rightarrow$ 22 Pylon.


* **Execution:** Do not move out until all 3 Zealots are fully built. Once your scouting Probe confirms the enemy base location, execute a coordinated probe-line raid.
* **Home Macro:** Maintain constant Probe production at home. Drop a Cybernetics Core (BY) at 24 supply.
* **Micro Tactics:** If you encounter 1 enemy Zealot + 1 Dragoon, focus down the enemy Zealot first and ignore the Dragoon. Push into their mineral line to target Probes. Forcing Probe kills and halting their production should net you a 4–6 worker economic lead.
* **Probe Preservation:** Keep your scouting Probe alive. Hide it behind the enemy's Natural expansion to monitor their expansion timings or technological transitions. If your Probe is picked off and you lose vision, play safe: drop a Robotics Facility (VR) before adding 3 more Gateways, using Observers to break down the ramp.
* **Adapting to Opponent Reactions:**
* *Enemy goes Fast Expansion:* Drop 3 Gateways, pump continuous Dragoons, bust down their Natural, and transition into a macro game.
* *Enemy goes Fast Dark Templar:* Place a Photon Cannon (BC) at your choke point and brace for Shuttle DT drops.
* *Enemy goes Fast Reaver:* Establish your Natural expansion first, then transition into Reaver production for a heavy macro match.


* **Golden Rules of PVP:**
1. Never cut Probe production.
2. Micro the 3 Zealots relentlessly to kill Probes.
3. Keep your scouting Probe alive; if it dies, send a replacement to hide behind their Natural.
4. Constantly monitor their tech via Probes, Observers, or Shuttles to apply direct hard-counters.
5. **The PvP Core Reality:** The player who safely expands first wins; the player who forces an uncalculated frontal attack loses.

## 🏗️ Lesson 3: Wall-ins, Anti-Cheese & Tactical Crises

### I. PvZ SimCity (Wall-In Architecture on 'Fighting Spirit')

* **Core Logic:** Position your Forge (BF) on the left and Gateway (BG) on the right; or Forge at the bottom and Gateway on top.
* **Spawn Point Configurations:**
* **5 O'Clock:** Standard SimCity layout.
* **11 O'Clock:** Standard SimCity layout.
* **1 O'Clock & 7 O'Clock (Fast Nexus Opener / Nexus First):** Rally your 8th Probe directly out to build a Pylon. Drop the Natural Nexus at 12 supply, halt Probe production temporarily to build a Forge (BF), drop a Photon Cannon (BC) and Gateway (BG) at 14 supply, then resume Probe production. If a Zergling rush arrives, add another Forge to complete a solid, impenetrable wall.
* **1-Gateway Opener:** Follow the standard Forge SimCity wall blueprints, with an exception at 1 o'clock for optimized build velocity.
* **2-Gateway Wall-Ins:** Specific structural setups for 1/7 o'clock and 11/5 o'clock positions to deny Zergling entry.



### II. PvT Wall-In Principles

* Deploy Pylons along the outermost edges of your main base to clear the fog of war and defend against Dropship plays. You can extend this Pylon grid to your 3rd and 4th bases.
* Group 4 Gateways together. Leave a single full grid line open between the upper and lower pairs. Keep tech structures like the Citadel of Adun (VC) separated from your production structures.
* Use Pylons at the Natural to leave only a single narrow exit, blocking Terran Vulture run-bys or allowing a fast Pylon plug if needed.

### III. Crisis Management: Dealing with a 5-Pool (Zerg 5D All-In)

* **Close Position Strategy:** Abandon your Natural expansion Forge and wall plans. Move defensive production into your Main base.
* **Micro Response:** If Zerglings are spotted, construct a dummy Photon Cannon at the Natural to stall, while starting a Pylon in your Main. Pull 4 Probes to block the main ramp. Drop a real Photon Cannon in your Main. Micro your Probes using mineral-clicking to kite and stall the Zerglings until the Cannon completes. Once safe, transition into a 2-Gateway Zealot counter-force to reclaim your Natural expansion.

### IV. PvZ: Countering 9-Pool Speedlings into 2-Hatchery Vision Denial

1. **Handling 9-Pool Speedlings via 1-Gateway Opener:** Instantly pull a Probe to build a Forge, lock down the choke point using standard SimCity, drop 1 Photon Cannon, and pull 2–3 Probes to support your Zealots in plugging the gap.
2. **Breaking 2-Hatchery Lair Vision Denial (Overlord Creep/Containment):**
* Maintain standard macro development (Stargate $\rightarrow$ +1 Ground Attack $\rightarrow$ Citadel opener). At the 5-minute mark, build a proactive second safety Cannon deeper inside your Natural.
* Around 6 minutes, send a few Zealots out to scout the Zerg army composition. If you spot Hydralisks, add 5–6 Cannons at home immediately. If you only see Zerglings, push past the midfield line to force them to dump resources into larvae/defensive structures, then pull your Zealots right back home to avoid being surrounded.
* At 6:10, your first Corsair must scout along the standard ground attack path toward the Zerg base. Simultaneously, drop 1 Cannon at your Natural front (totaling 3) and 2 Cannons in your main mineral line.
* **Scenario A:** If the Corsair spots Hydralisks on the path (Hydralisk Rush), cancel the 2 Main base Cannons and mass-produce Cannons at your Natural front.
* **Scenario B:** If no Hydralisks are found, the Zerg is teching to Mutalisks. Cancel the Natural front Cannon and add up to 3 Cannons in your Main in a triangular defensive formation, while placing 1–2 Cannons in your Natural mineral line.
* **Mid-Game Transition:** Continuously build up to 7 Corsairs, upgrade +1 Air Attack, and build up a Zealot + High Templar army at home. Merge an Archon. Do not push out until +1 Air Attack finishes and your ultimate army is assembled. Transition into Robotics (VR/VO) around 11 minutes for Observers.



### V. PvZ: Countering the 4-Hatchery / 6-Hatchery Turtle Defenses

1. **Tempo Control:** PvZ revolves around macro rhythm (economy, tech, upgrades, Gateway counts). Do not assume the game is won just because you wiped out an army or stormed a mineral line; Zerg production bounces back incredibly fast in the mid-game.
2. **The Supply Benchmark:** Protoss cannot out-produce a maxed Zerg. You must push out and exert heavy map pressure at roughly 120 supply to threaten the Zerg's 3rd or 2nd expansions.
3. **Optimal Early Composition:** Zealots + High Templars + Corsairs. Transition into Dragoons only after your Archon and Storm core is fully online.
4. **Maintaining Sky Control:** Maintain 6–7 Corsairs to pick off Overlords. If the Zerg turtles behind a Spore Colony + Lurker grid, do not force a direct frontal ground engage.
5. **Shuttle Drop Disruptions:** Use Shuttle drops to devastate Zerg worker lines. The optimal loadout is **2 Dark Templars + 2 High Templars**. Drop the 2 DTs into the Zerg main base; once the Zerg pulls their workers toward the Natural expansion to escape, use the High Templars to storm the escaping worker line for maximum value.
6. **Simultaneous Expansion:** While harassing, expand aggressively, secure your bases with Cannons, and maximize your tech upgrades. Skip Dragoons in the mid-game to save gas for pure Zealot + Templar production, and tech to an Arbiter Tribunal.
7. **Late-Game PvZ Core:** Hold your bases with a combination of Cannons and High Templar Storm zones. Control Zerg expansion attempts to win the war of attrition. Avoid reckless base-trades or blind ramp charges. Utilize drops, storms, Zealot-Templar-Archon comps, and Reaver + Shuttle setups.
8. **The 20-Minute Mark:** Place Photon Cannons inside your main base to delay late-game Zerg Doom Drops. Use slow-pushing Reavers paired with Dragoon-Storm compositions to systematically secure remaining mineral fields and lock down a late-game victory through strategic positional play.
