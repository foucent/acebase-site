      <h1 class="ab-hero__title">Delta Force settings</h1>
      <p class="ab-hero__excerpt">Delta Force fights happen across wide maps where a clean, high frame rate beats film-quality lighting, and most of the heavy graphics options ship switched on. Set your display and field of view first, then flatten the rest of the visuals so your aim is the only thing left to tune.</p>
