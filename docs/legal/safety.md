        <h3>✅ Use official channels</h3>
        <p>We only top up through the game's official in-game store or a licensed distributor. Never accept "too good to be true" off-platform deals.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="mg-games__guide-card">
        <h3>🔐 Protect your account</h3>
        <p>Only share the Player ID / character ID needed to complete a top-up. Never share your login password or two-factor codes with anyone.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="mg-games__guide-card">
        <h3>🚫 Avoid scam warning signs</h3>
        <p>Beware of sellers demanding off-platform payment, asking for your password, or promising "unlimited free currency". If it looks wrong, it is.</p>
      </div>

        <p>Refunds follow our <a href="/legal/return-policy/">Return Policy</a>. Digital top-ups are usually non-refundable once credited, so confirm your details before paying.</p>
