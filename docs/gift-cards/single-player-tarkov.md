# Single Player Tarkov (SPT)

SPT is a community single-player setup based on Escape from Tarkov for offline practice and mods — **not** a standalone SEAGM gift-card product. Official content still comes from Battlestate / platform channels; on SEAGM, see the **Escape from Tarkov** game page and **Steam Wallet** cards.

- Offline / mod-oriented practice (community project)
- Separate from official online accounts and services
- Buy the official game and editions via Battlestate or Steam
- Reference: [Escape from Tarkov · SEAGM](https://www.seagm.com/zh-hk/escape-from-tarkov)

!!! warning "Important"
    SEAGM does not sell an “SPT-only” card. Do not redeem third-party PINs on unknown sites. Steam / official purchases are also **non-refundable / non-returnable** (see seller terms).

## How to redeem?

1. Sign in to Steam
2. Open [Redeem a Steam Wallet Code](https://store.steampowered.com/account/redeemwalletcode)
3. Choose Redeem a Steam Gift Card or Wallet Code
4. Paste the code from SEAGM **My Cards** and confirm
5. Once credited, buy games / DLC / in-app items on Steam
