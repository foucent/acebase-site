# Call of Duty: Warzone

Large-scale free battle royale / tactical shooter, often linked with modern Call of Duty content. SEAGM has no dedicated Warzone card; PC spends often use **Battle.net Balance Card (US)**. Call of Duty **Mobile** CP codes are a different product from Warzone on PC/console.

- Large matches and loadout progression
- Buy related digital content via Battle.net balance
- Battle.net account region / currency must match the card
- Catalog: [SEAGM Game Cards](https://www.seagm.com/zh-hk/card?code=game-cards)

!!! warning "Important"
    Battle.net Balance Card (US) works only on accounts **registered in the US** with matching store currency. Purchases are **non-refundable and non-returnable**.

## How to redeem?

1. Open [battle.net/balance](https://www.battle.net/balance)
2. Sign in to Battle.net
3. Enter the code in the redeem field
4. Confirm — balance credits on success

!!! note "Usage limits"
    Battle.net balance usually cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or combined with other payment methods; see Battle.net terms for details.
