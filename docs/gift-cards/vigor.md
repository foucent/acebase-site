# Vigor

Post-apocalyptic survival extraction — loot the Outlands, strengthen your shelter, and fight others for resources. SEAGM **Game Cards** has no dedicated Vigor card; on console, use the matching store balance / gift card (Xbox / PlayStation, etc.).

- Loot–extract–base-building loop
- Free-to-play with IAP for progress and cosmetics
- Pick a gift card that matches your platform and region
- Catalog: [SEAGM Game Cards](https://www.seagm.com/zh-hk/card?code=game-cards)

!!! warning "Important"
    Platform gift cards are usually region-locked and **non-refundable / non-returnable**. Confirm your account region before buying.

## Available denominations

No Vigor-specific price table. Choose a gift card that matches your platform and region in SEAGM’s game-card category; avg prices are collected on a schedule.

---

## How to redeem?

1. Open the gift card / balance redeem page in your platform store
2. Sign in to your platform account
3. Enter the PIN to top up
4. Return to Vigor for IAP or content unlocks

---

## Purchase & delivery (SEAGM example)

1. Open [SEAGM Game Cards](https://www.seagm.com/zh-hk/card?code=game-cards)
2. Search for and select your platform / region gift card
3. After payment, check the PIN under **My Cards**
4. Redeem via the platform flow
