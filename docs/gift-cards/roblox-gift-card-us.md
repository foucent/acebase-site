# Roblox Gift Card (US)

US-region digital gift card that adds credit to a Roblox account. Use it for Robux, Premium, in-game items, and avatar gear. After redeeming you get Credit for game expansions, avatar items, or Premium.

- Redeeming a gift card often includes virtual item rewards
- Credit works for avatar outfits and in-game boosts
- A convenient digital gift for Roblox players
- Play free games on PC, phone, tablet, Xbox, and more

!!! warning "Important"
    From **April 24, 2023**, Roblox gift cards sold here redeem only in the currency set on your **Roblox account**. Purchases are **non-refundable and non-returnable**.

## How to redeem?

1. Open Roblox **Game Card Redemption**
2. Enter the gift card **PIN**
3. Click **Redeem** to credit the signed-in account
4. You get a success message; balance appears next to **Your Balance** (usually in green)

!!! note "Currency conversion"
    After redeeming, credit converts to your Roblox account currency. Confirm region / currency before redeeming.
