Inspired by [MyGear Guide · Shop](https://guide.mygear.top/). AceBase focuses on **top-up price compare**. For real orders use **Crisp chat**.

## Steps

### 1. Send your request

Include in one message:

```text
Game / region:
Denomination or bundle:
Account info (Player ID / login method as required):
Payment preference (Wise / other):
Notes:
```

### 2. Confirm quote

Support replies with **total price including fees/delivery notes** and ETA. If stock or region mismatches, we say so upfront and help adjust.

### 3. Pay

Pay the confirmed amount (Wise recommended). Add **order keyword / nickname** in the payment note for reconciliation.

### 4. Delivery

After payment confirmation, we complete top-up or send codes per [Delivery](shipping-and-delivery.md).

!!! tip "Tip"
    Product pages are reference only — **final price is from support**. Avoid high-risk channels on your own.
