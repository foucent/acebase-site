# Shipping Policy

<p class="ab-meta">Structure inspired by [BitTopup Shipping Policy](https://bittopup.com/bittopup_shipping_policy.html) · AceBase Demo wording</p>

AceBase focuses on **digital delivery** (account credit, PIN / CDK codes). No physical parcels by default (knife merch Demo may differ — confirm with support).

## Delivery methods

| Type | Method |
| --- | --- |
| Game top-up | Credited after payment confirmation |
| PIN / gift / CDK codes | Sent via WhatsApp / email / support |

See also: [Shipping & delivery](../shop/shipping-and-delivery.md)

## Regions

Digital goods can be ordered across regions, but confirm **server / country eligibility** (wrong region may be unusable).

## Fees

Digital delivery has **no shipping fee**.

## Contact

Delivery status: [Contact](contact.md) or your order channel.

!!! note "Demo"
    Formal SLA (promised credit time) is agreed with support until production launch.
