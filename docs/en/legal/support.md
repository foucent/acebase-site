# Support

We help with accounts, orders, and product usage questions.

## What we can help with

- **Ordering & payment**: quotes, payment notes, reconciliation  
- **Delivery issues**: not received, wrong region/ID (most direct top-ups cannot be reversed — verify IDs first)  
- **Product notes**: denomination meaning, region limits, redeem tips  
- **Feedback**: page bugs, UX issues  

## How to reach us

Use [Contact](contact.md) or [How to order](../shop/how-to-order.md). Include your order keyword and screenshots when possible.
