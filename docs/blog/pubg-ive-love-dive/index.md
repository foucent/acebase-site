      <h1 class="ab-article__title">PUBG × IVE Love Dive: Three Rondo Zones, Six Idols, a November Launch</h1>
      <p class="ab-article__meta"><time datetime="2026-10-28">28 Oct 2026</time> &middot; PUBG</p>
    </header>

    <p class="ab-article__standfirst">KRAFTON&rsquo;s crossover with IVE puts three LOVE DIVE areas on Rondo and all six members in game as outfits, with themed clothing, armour, weapon skins and emotes. PC on 4 November, consoles on 12 November.</p>

    <figure class="ab-article__figure">
      <img src="/assets/news/pubg-ive-love-dive/cover.jpg" alt="The six members of IVE in school uniforms among ruined statues, under pink chrome LOVE DIVE lettering and the PUBG Battlegrounds x IVE logos" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
    </figure>

    <div class="ab-article__body">
      <p>PUBG&rsquo;s latest collaboration is with IVE, built around the group&rsquo;s single &ldquo;LOVE DIVE&rdquo;. KRAFTON has dated it: PC on 4 November, consoles on 12 November.</p>
      <p>The collab&rsquo;s centre is Rondo, which picks up three new areas designed from the scenes and visual language of the LOVE DIVE music video.</p>
      <p>All six members appear in game, each as their own outfit. The outfits come with themed clothing, accessories, armour and weapon skins, and the collab adds emotes built around IVE&rsquo;s music.</p>
      <p>A full trailer premieres on 1 November with all six on screen. The first teaser, out with the announcement, already shows the new map areas and a new arrangement that sets &ldquo;LOVE DIVE&rdquo; over PUBG&rsquo;s own theme.</p>
      <p>What the announcement does not say is how the items are earned &mdash; no pass, crate or event schedule has been named &mdash; or when the collab ends. Those two answers should come with the trailer on 1 November.</p>
      <p>PUBG&rsquo;s PC and console store runs on G-Coin; the reference price for a top-up is on our <a href="/games/#pubg-gcoin">PUBG G-COIN page</a>.</p>
      <p class="ab-article__source">Source: KRAFTON announcement and first teaser, 28 October 2026.</p>
