      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Gladiator: Blades of Fury is a close-quarters fighting game from Yash Future Tech Solutions, set in the medieval arena rather than anywhere with firearms. The fights are meant to feel heavy: steel on steel, no ranged shortcut.</p>
    <p>Four tribes — barbarian, assassin, knight and dwarf — field twenty heroes between them, each with its own fighting style and weapon. Combat turns on weapon combos and slow-motion dodge counters, where reading an attack and stepping away from it is what opens the finish.</p>
    <p>Seven arenas host the fights, mountain snow among them. Tournament mode is the campaign of the game, fought for the title of champion gladiator; quick match is where you take a character or a map you have not learned yet and find out how it plays.</p>
