      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Colleccct is middlepattern's incremental action game. Your craft fires on its own at abstract shapes drifting across the screen; break one and it bursts into glowing motes. Collect the motes, spend them, and the next shape breaks faster than the last.</p>
    <p>Upgrades happen live. There is no pause between the action and the menu: fire rate goes up, the craft itself can grow to sweep in more motes, the scene can change to one that pays out more. As the numbers climb, a simple-looking screen turns into a chain of destruction with light coming off it.</p>
    <p>The game came to Steam from itch.io on Apr 3, 2026 and has been well received. The developer describes it as an hour-long experience — a short run built around the feeling of efficiency improving in front of you.</p>
    <p>On Oct 27 the developer announced the move to a $1 price, and is sorting the change out with Valve now; no date has been given. The same update added achievements and basic controller support for Steam Deck, and removed the in-game merchandise store, which had been meant to fund development and did not. New Game+ is planned for later.</p>
