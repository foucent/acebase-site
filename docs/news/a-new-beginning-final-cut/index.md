      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>A New Beginning: Final Cut is a point-and-click adventure from Daedalic Entertainment, and it presents itself as a graphic novel: more than an hour of animated comic panels carries the story between the puzzles, rather than the usual talking heads.</p>
    <p>Fay is a young woman from a future in which the environment has already collapsed. She travels back to find Bent Svensson, a retired bioengineer whose work on alternative energy is the last thing that could stop the collapse — and whose research is about to end up in the hands of an energy company that would rather profit from the crisis than prevent it.</p>
    <p>The two travel the world together, exploring locations, collecting clues and solving puzzles to take apart the company's plan. HowLongToBeat puts a main-story run at about nine and a half hours, and a completionist one at around ten and a half.</p>
    <p>The giveaway lasted exactly 24 hours and closed on Nov 2. Copies claimed inside that window stay in the library; the store page below is now only useful to buy the game outright.</p>
