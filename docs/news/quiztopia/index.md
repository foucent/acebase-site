      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Quiztopia: The Ultimate Trivia is a knowledge quiz from Yash Future Tech Solutions, written for players from five years old up — the kind of game that is played sitting next to someone rather than alone with headphones.</p>
    <p>Questions come in six categories: maths, science, geography, general knowledge, English and pictures. Each category holds ten levels, each level ten questions, and the difficulty climbs as the levels go, so a category takes a while to run dry.</p>
    <p>Categories are picked by interest rather than in one fixed order, which is what makes it work as a family quiz: the player who likes the picture rounds never has to sit through the arithmetic to get to them.</p>
