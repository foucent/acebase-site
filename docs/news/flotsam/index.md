      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Flotsam is an ocean survival and colony builder. There is no land left worth the name: the game opens on a single endless sea, and everything the colony stands on has to come out of it.</p>
    <p>You fish floating plastic and driftwood out of the water, salvage what the old world left behind, and put workers on the jobs that keep a settlement going — floating houses first, then the systems that make living there possible. The base expands outward, and the ocean beyond it is what pays for the next round of research.</p>
    <p>The survival loop is where the game gets its teeth. Water has to be desalinated, contaminated seafood purified, and the pollution kept from turning into disease. Fuel starts as wood and can be moved up to what the game calls green eel power, until the colony is running its own small ecosystem.</p>
    <p>The same giveaway also includes the Morte's Champions of Renown Pack for Idle Champions of the Forgotten Realms. The base game there is free to play; the pack has to be claimed from inside the game once you have signed in through Epic, so it is not a copy that lands in the library on its own.</p>
