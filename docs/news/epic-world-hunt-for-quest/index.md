      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Yash Future Tech Solutions' game is a horizontal action adventure: an orc king has taken the gold, and you are the adventurer walking into his country to carry it back out. The way through is platforming — traps, moving machinery, and the timing that gets you past them.</p>
    <p>The campaign runs forty-two levels: seven worlds of six stages each, with the scenery changing from world to world. Three playable characters carry different weapons, and thirteen kinds of enemy are built to answer them differently, so a stage does not play the same way twice.</p>
    <p>Levels are seeded with coins and hidden chests, and there are more than sixteen kinds of trap between the start and the orc king's chest.</p>
