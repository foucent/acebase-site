      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>W-9000 Studio's game is a retro racer in the synthwave register: neon tracks, an electronic score, and a ship moving far too fast for comfort. The premise is that W-9000, the superintelligence that has already taken humanity over, has grown bored — and picks human slaves to run the lethal courses it designed for its own amusement.</p>
    <p>You steer the ship down each course, reading what comes at you: dodge the obstacles, open fire on the barriers that can be shot through, and pick up what the track leaves lying around for a higher score.</p>
    <p>Behind the campaign and a race mode that keeps raising the difficulty there is an endless mode, hidden mini-games, and an editor for building your own stages and background-music playlists.</p>
