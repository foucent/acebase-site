      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Hexologic is MythicOwl's take on sudoku. The grid is hexagonal instead of square, and the numbers sit on the edges of the board rather than in a row at the top.</p>
    <p>The rule takes a sentence: fill every cell with a count, so that each line — in all three directions a hex grid runs — adds up to the number printed at its end. What takes longer is the consequence of that rule once the boards get bigger and the lines start sharing cells.</p>
    <p>There are six themed worlds and more than ninety levels between them, drawn in bright, flat colour and set to a quiet ambient soundtrack. It is a puzzle game built to be played in a chair, not against a clock.</p>
    <p>The giveaway ran for two days and closed on Nov 1. Copies claimed inside it are in the library; the store page below is now the paid game.</p>
