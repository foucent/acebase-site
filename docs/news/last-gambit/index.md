      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Last Gambit, from Trabucco team, is chess where the board is a run. You do not begin with an army the way you would in a normal game — you build one as you climb, and the pieces you find are not all pieces chess has a name for.</p>
    <p>There are twenty-seven of them. A knight can grow into a unicorn; a queen can step off the edge of the board; a phoenix comes back after it falls. Pieces fuse and duplicate, so a run drifts away from anything a chess opening could predict.</p>
    <p>The single-player story moves up a branching map of the kind a tower climb uses, with merchants and bosses at the end of chapters. Battles also hand out cards with their own effects: undo the last move, freeze a piece in place, move one to another square, bring a fallen piece back.</p>
    <p>Online play runs two rule sets. The fixed-roster mode gives both players the same random pool, lets each ban one piece, and then leaves the rest to be arranged freely. The budget mode gives a fixed number of points and a price on every piece, so the choice is between a few expensive elites and a crowd.</p>
    <p>The team's stated reason for charging is that it does not want the game to sell wins, run ads or take a subscription, and a price is what keeps the studio running.</p>
