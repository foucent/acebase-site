      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What it is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>Asleep: Act 1 is the first of three acts from Black Hole Games — a 2D point-and-click survival horror game told from inside a single character's nightmare.</p>
    <p>Ana Lúcia is lost in a version of her own thoughts that has turned hostile. The clues are in her sketchbook, and the puzzles come out of what she has drawn there. The only tool she carries is a torch, and the game is built so that the torch is not a comfort.</p>
    <p>Light is the mechanic. Different enemies react to it in opposite ways — some keep away from it, some hunt it — so using the torch is a decision about which of those you want to meet. Stay in the dark too long and a sanity meter starts to fall: the anxiety sets in first, and the nightmare around her grows more chaotic as it does.</p>
    <p>The giveaway ran for three days and closed on Nov 3. Copies claimed before then are in the library; the store page below is now the paid game.</p>
