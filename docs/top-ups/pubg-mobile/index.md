      <h2 class="ab-section__title">The game</h2>
    </div>
    <p>PUBG Mobile is a mobile battle royale game built around tactical survival. Players drop into a battlefield, gather supplies, use terrain and vehicles to their advantage, and fight the rest of the lobby to be the last one standing.</p>
    <p>Beyond the classic battle royale mode, the game keeps adding new maps, new modes, cosmetic systems and seasonal content, so there is always something new to play for.</p>
    <p>For anyone who wants more cosmetic content, a personalised account and the season's rewards, UC is one of the most important resources in the game.</p>

  <section class="ab-section" id="what-is-uc">
    <div class="ab-section__head">
      <h2 class="ab-section__title">What UC is</h2>
    </div>
    <p>UC is the premium in-game currency of PUBG Mobile. It is not sold inside the match; it is bought with real money and then spent on the things free play does not unlock.</p>
    <h3>Cosmetics</h3>
    <p>Character skins, weapon skins, vehicle skins, outfits and appearance accessories, emotes, voice packs and the rest of the personalisation content.</p>
    <h3>Royale Pass and seasonal rewards</h3>
    <p>UC buys or upgrades the Royale Pass, which is how a season's pass rewards and its exclusive content are unlocked.</p>
    <h3>Crates, bundles and event items</h3>
    <p>Special bundles, limited-time event items, crate contents and other premium resources are priced in UC as well.</p>
    <p>In short: if you want your account to look different, to collect limited rewards and to stand out on the battlefield, UC is the currency that pays for it.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="ab-section" id="how-to-order">
    <div class="ab-section__head">
      <h2 class="ab-section__title">How to order</h2>
    </div>
    <p>UC cannot usually be earned in any consistent way through free play, so a top-up is the practical route. A top-up credits UC to the player ID (UID) of an existing account — nothing is installed, and no password is needed.</p>
    <ol>
      <li>Pick a package above and press ORDER NOW. The package and its price go into our live chat.</li>
      <li>Send your PUBG Mobile UID in the same chat, plus the region or server you play on, so we can confirm that the package can be delivered to your account.</li>
      <li>We confirm availability and the final total, and give you the payment details in the chat.</li>
      <li>Once the order is processed, the UC is credited to the UID you sent. Check the game to see the balance.</li>
    </ol>
    <p><strong>Check the UID before you pay.</strong> Once an order is in progress it is much harder to change, and UC sent to the wrong account cannot be pulled back.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="ab-section" id="faq">
    <div class="ab-section__head">
      <h2 class="ab-section__title">FAQ</h2>
    </div>
    <h3>What is PUBG Mobile UC used for?</h3>
    <p>UC is the premium currency in PUBG Mobile. It buys skins, outfits, vehicle cosmetics, emotes and voice packs and bundles, and it unlocks or upgrades the Royale Pass.</p>
    <h3>What information do you need to top up?</h3>
    <p>Usually just the correct PUBG Mobile UID and the UC package you want. Tell us the region or server you play on as well, so we can confirm the package can be delivered.</p>
    <h3>Where do I find my PUBG Mobile UID?</h3>
    <p>Log in to the game, tap your profile avatar or player profile icon, and the Player ID / UID is on the profile page.</p>
    <h3>How long does a UC top-up take?</h3>
    <p>Once payment is complete and the order has been processed, UC is normally credited as soon as possible. The actual time depends on the order, and the chat stays open until it lands.</p>
    <h3>Can I top up UC for a friend?</h3>
    <p>Generally yes. The order goes to whichever UID you send, so a friend's player ID works the same way as your own.</p>
    <h3>What if I sent the wrong UID?</h3>
    <p>Message us as soon as you notice. Once the order has entered processing it can become much harder to change, which is why the UID is worth double-checking before paying.</p>
